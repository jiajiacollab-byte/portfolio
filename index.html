About

From clinical practice to project management and marketing

I started out training in dental medicine, not project management. But while running online and in-person dental education programs — coordinating instructors, planning launches, writing the marketing, and managing the budget — I realized what actually energized me wasn't the clinical content itself. It was the work of pulling a project together: solving problems in real time, aligning people with very different priorities, and turning something complex into something that runs smoothly.

My first project was a modest online course on a niche clinical topic. I learned the fundamentals there — how to position a course, write copy that converts, and manage a paid ad campaign from a near-empty toolkit. That project became the foundation for everything that came after: online courses with stronger positioning and performance, and eventually premium in-person programs with multiple industry sponsors and demanding logistics — the kind of projects where a single missed detail can throw off an entire event.

Today I work across the full lifecycle of a course or campaign: project planning and timeline management, stakeholder and vendor coordination, marketing strategy and copywriting, paid advertising, and on-site execution. I'm now looking to bring that same skill set to project management, marketing, and healthcare education roles beyond the dental space — including international and remote opportunities.

  • 01 Adaptability Moved from clinical dental practice into project management and digital marketing, applying a fast learning curve to new domains.
  • 02 Cross-functional communication Coordinating between instructors, vendors, and marketing teams to keep projects aligned and on schedule.
  • 03 International outlook Based in Vienna, Austria, working comfortably across Chinese- and English-language professional contexts.
