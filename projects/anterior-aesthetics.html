Anterior Aesthetics Course

Launching a premium in-person dental aesthetics program from 0 → 1, led by Dr. Li — sold out within 24 hours of opening registration.

My Role

Project Manager

Format

In-Person Course

Timeline

5-month prep · Course: Jun 6–8, 2026

Revenue

$31K USD

Audience

Dentists (16 seats)

Problem

The instructor, Dr. Li, had strong personal credibility but had never run a premium in-person course before. The program had to be built from zero — positioning, pricing, the enrollment funnel, and on-site delivery — all within a fixed 5-month window before the live course dates.

My Role

End-to-end project manager: timeline and milestone planning, marketing coordination, participant communication, budget oversight, and on-site execution across all 3 course days.

Action

Built the enrollment page and course positioning; ran a lean teaser-to-enrollment ad funnel (172 clicks at a $0.10 CPC, $120 total spend); coordinated designers and vendors; managed payment and pre-course communication; ran logistics and problem-solving on-site across the 3-day live program.

Result

All 16 seats sold out within 24 hours of opening registration, generating $31K in revenue. Demand from the waiting list justified a second cohort, which sold out in 41 days with no additional advertising spend.
