Dental Trauma Guide

Built demand for a low-frequency, low-perceived-urgency clinical topic, led by Dr. Zhang and Dr. Liao, by reframing it around practical relevance rather than technical depth.

Online dental education program · Sep – Nov 2025

16.5x Estimated ROAS
604 Best Campaign Clicks
$0.10 Best CPC

My Role

Project Manager / Marketing

Format

Online Course

Timeline

Sep – Nov 2025

Revenue

$3.8K USD

Audience

Dentists

Problem

Dental trauma is a lower-frequency clinical topic with less perceived urgency than more commonly discussed dental subjects, and the instructor team had already released other dental education programs, making differentiation harder.

My Role

Project management, content marketing, advertising management, and enrollment operations for the full campaign.

Action

Reframed the course around practical, real-world relevance rather than technical depth, to build demand for a topic dentists don't encounter daily.

Result

16.5x estimated return on ad spend, with the best campaign generating 604 clicks at a $0.10 CPC.
