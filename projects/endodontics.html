Marketing & Launch Owner

Format

Audience

Problem

The instructor had already released multiple endodontic education programs, and the topic itself — ultrasonic-assisted treatment — served a narrow, highly specialized audience. The course needed a clear point of differentiation to stand out in an already-saturated catalog.

My Role

Product positioning, landing page copywriting, social content, Meta advertising management, course setup, and enrollment support.

Action

Repositioned the course around practical clinical workflow gains rather than technical features, wrote the landing page and social content from scratch, and managed the Meta ad campaign end-to-end.

Result

52 orders at a 6.6x return on ad spend, with the best-performing creative reaching a $0.07 CPC.
