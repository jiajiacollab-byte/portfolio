My Role

Marketing & Launch Owner

Format

Problem

The instructor delivered finished course recordings but almost no marketing material. A highly technical, academic topic (occlusion) had to be turned into a clear, sellable product with no existing positioning or messaging to build on.

My Role

Full launch owner: landing page copywriting, social content, ad creative strategy, Meta advertising management, course setup, and enrollment support.

Action

Segmented the audience into two clinical skill levels, wrote the positioning and full landing page from scratch, and built a multi-touch ad funnel (content → teaser → enrollment → countdown) across Meta and LINE.

Result

92 orders at a 15.8x return on ad spend, reaching 84% of the campaign revenue target and a 7% order increase over the previous course launch.
